Listing Rules & Prohibited Items

All listings posted on ScrapXtrade must represent genuine, legally tradeable scrap material that the posting party owns, controls, or is duly authorised to offer for trade.

3.1 Listing Requirements

  • Accurate and truthful description of material grade, quantity, and condition.
  • Valid port of loading or delivery location as applicable.
  • A reachable and verified contact number (WhatsApp-active preferred).
  • Pricing in a recognized commercial format (per MT, KG, or Price on Request).

3.2 Prohibited Items

The following categories are strictly prohibited from being listed on ScrapXtrade:

  • Radioactive or nuclear waste materials of any type.
  • Hazardous chemical waste not classified for legal export/import under Basel Convention.
  • Medical or biological waste not compliant with applicable disposal regulations.
  • Stolen goods or materials with disputed provenance or unclear title.
  • Counterfeit or mis-graded materials intentionally misrepresented as premium grade.
  • Items subject to UN trade sanctions or embargoes against specific nations.
  • Live ammunition, weapons, explosives, or military materials of any kind.
  • PCB-contaminated transformers or equipment unless certified pre-treated.

ScrapXtrade reserves the right to remove any listing it deems in violation of these rules or applicable law, without notice, and to report such listings to the appropriate authorities where warranted.
