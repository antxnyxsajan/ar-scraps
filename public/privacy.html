Information We Collect

ScrapXtrade collects information necessary to provide, operate, and improve our B2B marketplace services. We collect information in the following categories:

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Registration Data: Mobile phone number, nature of business, company name when you register for an account.
  • Listing Data: Material title, category, quantity, price, location, description, and scrap photos you upload when posting trade offers or buy leads.
  • Inquiry Data: Your name, company, phone number, and message content when you submit trade inquiries to other traders.
  • Communication Data: Any direct communications you send to ScrapXtrade support via email, WhatsApp, or phone.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, listings viewed, search queries entered, filters applied, and time spent on various sections of the Platform.
  • Device & Technical Data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device model, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Cookie Data: Session identifiers, preference settings, and analytics identifiers stored in browser cookies or localStorage (see Section 6).
  • Referral Data: The URL or source that directed you to ScrapXtrade (search engine, social media, etc.).

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • We may receive basic profile information if you connect via third-party authentication services (if applicable in future).
  • Publicly available business registration or directory information used for trader verification purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following legitimate purposes:

Platform Operation: To create and manage your trader account, publish your listings, process inquiries, and facilitate connections between buyers and sellers.
Communication: To send you transactional confirmations, platform updates, trade alerts, and responses to your support inquiries.
Safety & Verification: To verify trader identity via OTP, detect and prevent fraud, spam listings, or misuse of the Platform.
Analytics & Improvement: To analyse Platform usage patterns and improve our features, search relevance, and overall user experience.
Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable Indian laws, respond to lawful governmental or regulatory requests, and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

We do not use your data for: selling to third-party advertisers, profiling for political or social scoring purposes, or any purpose materially different from those listed above without your prior consent.

Sharing of Information

ScrapXtrade does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

4.1 With Other Traders (Your Consent)

Your listing information — including company name, location, material details, and contact phone number — is publicly visible on the Platform to facilitate B2B trade. This is an inherent part of the marketplace function that you consent to when posting a listing.

4.2 With Service Providers

We may share data with trusted third-party service providers who assist in operating the Platform, such as cloud hosting providers, analytics services, and cybersecurity vendors. These providers are contractually bound to handle your data securely and solely for the purposes of providing services to ScrapXtrade.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or other governmental authority, or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of ScrapXtrade, our users, or the public.

4.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of all or part of ScrapXtrade's business, your data may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to the same privacy protections as described in this policy.

6

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

ScrapXtrade uses cookies and similar local storage technologies to enhance your experience on the Platform and to understand how the Platform is used.

6.1 Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for the Platform to function — session management, authentication tokens, and security cookies. These cannot be disabled.
Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the Platform — pages visited, time on site, and referral sources. Data is aggregated and anonymised. We may use Google Analytics or similar tools.
Preference Cookies: Remember your settings such as language preference, last-viewed categories, and filter selections to improve your return experience.

6.2 Managing Cookies

You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may impair your ability to use key features of the Platform. Most modern browsers allow you to:

  • View all cookies stored by websites.
  • Delete existing cookies individually or in bulk.
  • Block all cookies or cookies from specific websites.
  • Set preferences for future cookie handling.

Third-party services embedded in the Platform (such as analytics or map providers) may also set their own cookies, governed by their respective privacy policies.

Your Rights

As a registered trader or visitor of ScrapXtrade, you have the following rights with respect to your personal information, in accordance with applicable Indian data protection laws:

Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, including listing history and account information.

Right to Correction

Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.

Right to Deletion

Request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for communications at any time by contacting us. This does not affect processing already carried out.

Right to Restriction

Request restriction of processing of your data in certain circumstances, such as while a correction request is under review.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

Lodge a complaint with the appropriate Indian regulatory authority if you believe your data rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us in writing at support@scrapxtrade.com. We will respond within 30 days of receipt of your request. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.
